Common questions

How do I switch between ounces and milliliters?
Settings → Measurements, or the oz / ml buttons at the bottom of any recipe.
What is Bartending Mode?
Bigger text and buttons you can read at arm's length. Toggle it from the Bar Mode button on Search or in Settings.
How do I get a house drink onto the menu?
In Build, move it to On the Menu (drag it on the board, or swipe right in the list). It needs a name that's unique and a real ingredient in place of every template placeholder. Once it's on the menu, it appears in Search, Study, and Stats.
Where are my photos stored?
Only on your device, inside the app. See the Privacy Policy.
Can I see the intro again?
Settings → Show intro again.
