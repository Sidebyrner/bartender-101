House Pour

Privacy Policy

Effective September 17, 2026

The short version: House Pour doesn't collect, share, or sell any of your data. Everything you create stays on your device.

What the app stores

To work, House Pour saves the following only on your device, inside the app's own storage:

  • Your study progress and drill results
  • Your shift log (drinks you mark as made)
  • House drinks you create, including tasting notes and labels
  • Ingredients you add and your recently used ingredients
  • Photos you choose to take or add for a drink
  • Your preferences, such as units and Bartending Mode

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. We run no servers and have no accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking. If your device backs up to iCloud or your computer, this data may be included in that backup under Apple's terms, not ours.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you tap Take Photo on a drink. When you choose Choose from Library, the system photo picker gives the app just the photo you pick — House Pour can't see the rest of your library. Photos are saved in the app on your device, and you can delete them at any time.

Sharing

If you use the Share button on a photo, it goes wherever you choose to send it through the system share sheet. The app doesn't share anything on its own.

Deleting your data

You can clear progress, the shift log, house drinks, and photos from the app's Settings. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on the device.

Children

House Pour is about alcoholic drinks and is intended for adults of legal drinking age. It isn't directed at children, and it collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
